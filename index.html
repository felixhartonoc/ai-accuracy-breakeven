How this is calculated

Break-even accuracy = 1 − (review cost ÷ error cost). Above it, letting the model run costs less than having a human check everything. Below it, unreviewed automation costs more than full review.

No review = 1,000 × (1 − accuracy) × error cost.

Full review = 1,000 × review cost.

Targeted review sends the lowest-confidence share of outputs — a share equal to the model’s error rate — to a human, and assumes that tail catches 90% of the errors. It is the only strategy of the three that assumes a confidence signal actually exists.

The 90% capture figure is an assumption, not a measurement. If you have not calibrated a confidence signal on your own data, treat the targeted-review row as a number to earn rather than one you already have.
